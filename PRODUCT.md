# Postroom

## Register

product

## Users

A small business reading and writing private correspondence on office desktops and phones. Administrators manage accounts in the same app.

## Product Purpose

A limited, strict-free mail pilot with truthful delivery and capacity states. Routine users need no provider accounts.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, familiar, precise.

## Anti-references

No generic card dashboards, Google logos/authentication imitation, decorative navigation, fake charts, or unsupported mail modules.

## Design Principles

Follow the supplied DESIGN.md and MOBILE_UX.md. Preserve drafts and navigation. Use Gmail-familiar hierarchy with original Postroom identity. Expose limitations at the point of action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard alternatives, reduced motion, 320 px reflow, 48 px mobile controls. Physical-device evidence must be labeled separately.
