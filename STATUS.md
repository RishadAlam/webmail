## Remaining release gates

1. **Complete control plane:** resumable domain/DNS/routing/Worker/webhook provisioning with exact diff approval, domain verification/drift views, broader provider reconciliation and the remaining role/ownership/recovery lifecycle. Bounded import-incident visibility/retry is now implemented; it is not the entire reconciliation control plane. These are unfinished features, not merely missing credentials. Current supported routine mailbox operations are in-app; initial provider accounts, deployment/secrets and CLI owner bootstrap are explicit owner exceptions.
2. **Actual free accounts and deployment:** confirm Render/Neon/Cloudflare/Resend account eligibility, billing-stop settings, HTTPS/canonical host, Neon certificate verification, effective limits, cold starts and suspension behavior. Render Free cannot provide a production availability guarantee. No deployment occurred.
3. **Real email path:** controlled owner-address inbound MIME, durable D1 storage while PHP is unavailable, import recovery, real Resend acceptance/webhook/delivery/usage, suppression behavior, quota resets and Cloudflare Free CPU measurement. The documented Resend daily-window discrepancy remains unresolved. Keep external sending disabled.
4. **Recovery:** owner-held encryption/MFA recovery material, off-machine encrypted backup retention, actual Neon/D1 restore and provider/idempotency reconciliation. Owner disaster recovery cannot depend on an unavailable app. The local drill does not implement scheduled cloud backups or prove live restoration.
5. **Devices and remaining checklist cases:** physical iOS/Android keyboard, safe areas/rotation/gestures, install/standalone, VoiceOver/TalkBack/NVDA, native text scaling/browser zoom, worker update while editing and other explicitly unrun combinations in the UI report. Emulation and axe are not substitutes.
6. **Pilot feature limits:** bounded metadata/operator search rather than full-text encrypted body search; basic thread correlation; strict small-attachment limits without inline preview; no durable offline drafts, push or exact scheduled sending; public email-password recovery disabled. Export/Trash deletion retains independent delivery/backup copies and does not claim erasure from providers.

Next: finish the bounded provisioning/reconciliation and remaining software acceptance work, then connect owner-controlled credentials through the encrypted interface for live verification. Before any DNS/routing change, show the actual proposed diff and obtain explicit approval. Do not represent pending setup, inaccessible metrics or an untested service as healthy or production-ready.
