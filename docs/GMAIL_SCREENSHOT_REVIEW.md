## Additional completed mail workflows

- **Contacts and writing:** up to 500 chosen contacts and 50 private plain-text templates; 4,000-character signatures and 20,000-character template bodies. Signatures are inserted once into new drafts; templates append without discarding typed text. Values are escaped and private writing content is encrypted. No contact scraping from messages.
- **Attachments:** encrypted upload, remove, resume and authorized download. Outbound allowlist: PDF, PNG, JPEG, GIF, TXT; at most 10 files, 128 KiB combined raw files, and 256 KiB encoded whole MIME. Incoming parsing is bounded; complex/oversized structures fail safely while the original MIME stays available. Downloads preserve exact bytes, use safe response headers, fit long filenames on mobile and are excluded from the service-worker cache. Inline preview is not implemented.
- **Search:** a field-based search form for sender, recipient, subject, label, dates, folder, category, unread state and attachments, backed by bounded metadata operators `from:`, `to:`, `subject:`, `in:`, `is:`, `has:attachment`, `before:`, `after:` and quoted `label:` values. Existing terms survive reopening; applying the form is explicit and combined query limits are validated. This is not full-text encrypted body search or the complete Gmail query language.
- **Snooze:** persisted timestamps and return actions with Undo. Due mail returns on authorized mailbox activity/import; this is not an exact-time background alarm or a push notification.
- **Recovery:** unattempted queued sends resume the original immutable intent and reservation; attempted/ambiguous sends stay locked. Worker wake timeout accommodates slower starts. Acknowledged raw payloads remain recoverable for a bounded default 24 hours, count toward spool limits, and expire separately from never-expired pending accepted data. Redacted import incidents and bounded MFA-protected retries are visible in Delivery.
- **Login:** focused centered panel, clear labels/errors, password visibility, and a Forgot password disclosure for the implemented administrator-managed recovery process. Public signup and email reset links remain disabled.

## Live Chrome interface follow-up

At the user's request, the signed-in Gmail interface was also reviewed in Chrome on November 1. The review inspected navigation, Quick settings, General settings, advanced search and an empty composer. It did not open unread conversations, change account preferences, submit a search/filter, send mail or copy private content into project artifacts. The empty composer was closed without entering content.

The live view confirmed three useful interaction patterns: Quick settings groups a few high-frequency choices and links to the complete settings page; advanced search exposes labeled fields with one explicit submit action; desktop compose keeps recipients and subject above an uncluttered writing surface with persistent bottom actions. WebMail already follows the first and third patterns. The field-based search form was added after this review, with safe quoting, scope restoration, accessible validation, a clear-fields action and responsive layouts. WebMail retains its own branding, original fictional fixtures, small-screen full-page composition and the verified features of its architecture.

## Validation and production boundary

The local milestone passes 297 PHP tests (16 intentional skips, 2,153 assertions), 28 local Worker tests, 10 frontend state/search tests and 144 browser workflows (48 each in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit). Static analysis, types, lint/format and production assets pass. Manual review covers desktop/mobile inbox, composer, settings, long attachment names and a dark lower reading pane. Executed commands and evidence are in [the UI report](UI_REPORT.md) and [STATUS.md](../STATUS.md).

This does not establish full Gmail parity or production readiness. Google AI, Chat/Meet/Workspace integrations, POP/IMAP, private offline mail and exact-time send/Undo Send remain outside the delivered scope. The complete domain/DNS/routing/Worker/webhook provisioning wizard and broader provider reconciliation are still unfinished software. Live controlled-address mail, owner credentials/free-account availability, Worker Free CPU, hosted PostgreSQL/TLS, cold starts, off-machine restore and physical-device/assistive-technology checks remain release gates. No external service was deployed or real mail sent.
